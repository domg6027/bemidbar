PRIVACY NOTICE

DERECH BEMIDBAR respects the privacy of visitors, enquirers and subscribers. Personal information is handled with care and is used for the administration and delivery of this service.

This notice explains what information may be collected, why it is needed, how it is stored, and how it is protected.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE?

DERECH BEMIDBAR operates under DERECH OLAM MISHKAN. The service is responsible for handling personal information collected directly for enquiries, subscriptions and subscriber administration.

2. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

Depending on your enquiry or subscription, the following information may be collected:

  • Your name.
  • Your WhatsApp contact number.
  • Your email address, where supplied or made available through payment processing.
  • Your country of residence, where needed for administration.
  • Payment confirmations and relevant subscription records.
  • Messages and correspondence relating to your enquiry or subscription.

We do not ask subscribers to provide unnecessary personal information.

3. WHY WE USE THIS INFORMATION

Personal information is used only where necessary to:

  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Administer subscriptions and payments.
  • Confirm subscriber status and payment records.
  • Deliver the weekly Shabbat teachings privately through WhatsApp.
  • Communicate relevant service information to subscribers.
  • Maintain appropriate financial and administrative records.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

4. HOW INFORMATION IS STORED

Subscriber records, including names, WhatsApp numbers, email addresses and payment confirmations, are kept in a private Google Drive.

RESTRICTED ACCESS

Access to these subscriber records is restricted to the person responsible for administering DERECH BEMIDBAR. Subscriber records are not kept in a publicly accessible folder or repository.

Appropriate care is taken to protect records against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES

DERECH BEMIDBAR uses external services to operate its website, communicate with subscribers and receive payments. These services have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

  • WhatsApp: Used for enquiries, subscriber communication and private delivery of recorded teachings. WhatsApp is operated by Meta.
  • PayPal: Used to receive international subscription payments. Payment information is processed by PayPal under its own privacy and security arrangements.
  • Banking services: South African subscribers may pay by direct bank transfer. Relevant payment information may be processed by the banks involved.
  • GitHub Pages: Used to host the public DERECH BEMIDBAR website. GitHub may process technical information associated with website visits under its own policies.
  • Google Drive: Used for private storage of subscriber administration records.

These external providers operate independently. Their own privacy policies apply to information they process.

6. SHARING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

Subscriber information is not sold, rented or made publicly available.

Personal information is not shared with unrelated parties for their marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by the service providers identified above, or disclosed where required by applicable law or a lawful authority.

7. HOW LONG INFORMATION IS KEPT

Personal information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for subscription administration, communication, financial record-keeping and applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or disposed of where appropriate, subject to any legal requirement to retain it.

8. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about the personal information held about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or raise concerns about how your information is being handled.

You may also request deletion of information where it is no longer required and there is no lawful reason to retain it.

Requests can be submitted through the DERECH BEMIDBAR WhatsApp contact channel.

9. SOUTH AFRICAN PRIVACY LAW

DERECH BEMIDBAR aims to handle personal information in accordance with applicable privacy legislation, including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), where applicable.

Nothing in this notice is intended to limit any rights available to individuals under applicable privacy law.

10. CONTACT REGARDING PRIVACY

For questions, requests or concerns relating to personal information, please contact DERECH BEMIDBAR through WhatsApp.

WhatsApp only. No voice calls or video calls are accepted. Please send a written WhatsApp message.
