WHY ARE WE ENTERING THE WILDERNESS?

The name BEMIDBAR means "in the Wilderness". It takes us back to the time when YHVH brought Yisra’el out of Mitsrayim and taught His people how to live as a people set apart for Him.

In the Wilderness, Yisra’el received instruction, learned dependence upon YHVH, and was shown how to order life according to His commands.

The Wilderness was not simply a geographical location. It was a place of instruction, testing, correction, and preparation.

That is the spirit in which we approach this journey: not to recreate the past, but to return to the foundations YHVH gave us and learn from His Davar.

"Stand in the ways and see, and ask for the old paths, where the good way is, and walk in it; then you shall find rest for your beings."
— Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 6:16, YAH Scriptures

RESTORING THE ANCIENT PATH

Across generations, religious traditions, interpretations, customs, and practices have accumulated around the Scriptures. Some may preserve valuable lessons; others may obscure the original instruction.

We must therefore be willing to examine what we have been taught. We must distinguish between what YHVH has commanded, what Scripture records, what people have practised, and what later traditions have added.

Restoration does not mean accepting something merely because it is old. Nor does it mean rejecting something merely because it is familiar. Everything must be examined in the light of the Davar.

Our foundation is the Davar.

We do not place tradition above Scripture, personal preference above instruction, or human authority above the commands of YHVH.

HEARING AND OBEYING

Learning the Way involves more than listening to a teaching or collecting knowledge. The instruction must reach our hearts and become visible in the way we live.

We are called to hear, understand, examine ourselves, and respond through obedience.

Yahusha HaMashiach taught His disciples to keep the words He had given them. His teaching does not invite us to treat obedience as an optional addition to faith.

"If you love Me, you shall guard My commands."
— Yohanan (John) 14:15, YAH Scriptures

WHAT WILL WE LEARN?

The Wilderness Journey will take us through teachings that examine the foundations of our faith and the practical implications of walking according to the Davar.

The subjects will be approached through Scripture, careful examination, and a desire to understand the instructions YHVH has given.

We will not approach every subject with the assumption that we already have the answers. We must be prepared to learn, reconsider, and correct our understanding wherever the Davar requires it.
