SUBSCRIPTIONS

Enter the Wilderness with us. DERECH BEMIDBAR offers a weekly recorded Shabbat teaching, returning to the Way YHVH taught His people.

Each teaching is prepared and recorded for the Shabbat, providing an opportunity to study the Davar, examine the Ancient Path, and learn to walk in obedience.

WHAT YOUR SUBSCRIPTION INCLUDES

  • One new recorded Shabbat teaching each week.
  • Approximately 30–40 minutes per teaching.
  • At least 52 weekly teachings throughout the annual subscription.
  • Special Shabbat teachings included without additional charge.
  • Private delivery to subscribers through WhatsApp as MP3 document attachments.

These are prepared recordings, not live broadcasts or livestreams. Paid subscriber recordings are delivered privately and are not published on the public website.
