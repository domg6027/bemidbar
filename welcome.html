WELCOME TO DERECH BEMIDBAR

DERECH BEMIDBAR is an invitation to journey back to the foundations of our faith: to listen again to the Davar of YHVH, to examine what we have inherited, and to learn the Way that YHVH established for His people.

The Wilderness was not merely a place through which Yisra’el travelled. It was also the place where YHVH instructed His people, established His dwelling among them, taught them obedience, and showed them how to walk in His ways.

We therefore enter this Wilderness Journey with a desire to learn — not according to the traditions of men, but by returning to the instructions and foundations given by YHVH.

Our guiding purpose:

To restore the Ancient Path by returning to the Davar, examining the Scriptures carefully, and learning to walk in obedience to YHVH.

WHY THE WILDERNESS?

The Wilderness reminds us that YHVH does not leave His people without instruction. He teaches, corrects, provides, protects, and leads.

It is a place of learning and preparation. It reminds us that following YHVH requires more than hearing words. It requires a willingness to receive His instruction and put it into practice.

Our journey is therefore not an exercise in nostalgia. It is a deliberate return to the foundations — allowing the Davar to examine our beliefs, our practices, and the paths on which we walk.

ENTERING THE WILDERNESS TO LEARN THE WAY

One Shabbat teaching at a time. One step at a time. Returning to the Ancient Path.

THE SHABBAT TEACHINGS

Each Shabbat, DERECH BEMIDBAR presents a recorded teaching of approximately 30–40 minutes.

These teachings are prepared for listening as Shabbat begins. They are intended to provide time for focused instruction, reflection, and prayer as we enter the set-apart day.

Each teaching is delivered as an MP3 recording. Special Shabbat teachings prepared for DERECH BEMIDBAR are included as part of the service.

This is a continuing journey of learning. We do not gather merely to collect information, but to hear the Davar and consider how we are to walk in its light.
