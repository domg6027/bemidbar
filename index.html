WELCOME TO THE WILDERNESS

DERECH BEMIDBAR is an invitation to return to the Ancient Path and learn again from the instructions YHVH gave His people in the Wilderness.

The Wilderness was not merely a place of wandering. It was where YHVH instructed Yisra'el, established His dwelling among them, and taught them how to walk in His Way.

Through Scripture-based recorded Shabbat teachings, we examine the Davar, consider the ancient foundations of our faith, and explore what it means to walk in obedience today.

This is not a replacement for DERECH OLAM. It is an opportunity to enter the BEMIDBAR — the Wilderness — and learn the Way more deeply.

HEAR THE DAVAR. LEARN THE WAY. WALK IN OBEDIENCE.

A journey of restoration through Scripture and the Ancient Path.

THE SHABBAT TEACHING

One new recorded teaching each Shabbat, prepared to encourage careful study, understanding and obedience to the Davar.

Each weekly teaching is approximately 30–40 minutes long and is delivered as an MP3 recording.

The teaching is intended to open Shabbat with the Davar. Special Shabbat teachings produced for the service are included in the subscription without an additional charge.

Active subscribers receive their weekly recordings privately through individual WhatsApp delivery.
